NeuralPLSI

NeuralPLSI models an outcome as g(X′β) + Z′γ. The exposures X (a mixture) enter through one weighted index X′β, with β of unit length and a positive first weight, and an unknown function g learned by a small neural network. The covariates Z enter linearly. The outcome can be continuous, binary (logistic) or a survival time (Cox). A pairs bootstrap gives standard errors and percentile intervals. Method: Do, Wang, Liu and Lee (2025), arXiv:2512.11593.

Everything runs on this device: the C++ engine is compiled to WebAssembly and runs in Web Workers in your browser. Data you open are not uploaded.

1Data

The simulation design of the paper: 8 exposures with correlation 0.3, three covariates, β ∝ (1, 0.7, −0.5, 0.5, 0.3, −0.1, 0, 0) and γ = (1, −0.5, 0.5). The generator is seeded but does not reproduce the draws of the Python package.

Log triglyceride of 800 NHANES participants against 8 exposures (α- and γ-tocopherol, retinyl stearate, retinol, two PCBs, a furan, trans-β-carotene), adjusted for age, sex and race. Prepared as in reproduce/nhanes.py: exposures logged (except retinol) and standardized. Selecting it sets the activation to ELU and adds an intercept, as that script does.

Data: supplementary material of Wang et al. (2020), Environmental Health 19:96, licensed CC BY 4.0.

Covariates (linear)

Rows with a missing or non-numeric value in a chosen column are dropped. Log drops non-positive values. Standardizing uses the mean and SD of the remaining rows. Text covariates are coded as indicators against their first level. Set the outcome type with Family under Model.

2Model

What these do

Family sets the loss: squared error, logistic likelihood or Cox partial likelihood. It follows the simulated outcome and is continuous for NHANES. Activation, hidden units and hidden layers shape the network for g. Weight decay is the L2 penalty on the network; larger values give a smoother g. Full-batch L-BFGS runs until the objective stops improving or max iterations; γ is then fitted exactly. g is anchored at g(0) = 0, so an intercept carries the outcome level (not identifiable for Cox). The seed sets the initialization and the bootstrap resamples.

SplinePLSI (Wang et al., 2020) replaces the network with a cubic B-spline with 5 knots and is fitted on the same data and bootstrap resamples, for comparison.

3Run

Fit uses all rows. Each bootstrap replicate resamples the rows with replacement and refits from a fresh initialization; standard errors are the SD of the draws and intervals their 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles. Replicates run in parallel, one per worker.
